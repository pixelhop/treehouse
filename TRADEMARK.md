# Treehouse Trademark Guidelines

Treehouse is open source software licensed under the GNU Affero General Public
License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0-only). The **source code** is free to use, modify, and
redistribute under those terms.

The **name "Treehouse", the Treehouse logo, and related brand assets** are
trademarks of Pixelhop and are **not** covered by the AGPL-3.0 license. Open
source licenses grant rights to the copyrighted software; they do **not** grant
any rights to use a project's trademarks. These guidelines explain what is and
is not permitted so that the community can rely on the Treehouse name to mean
the software Pixelhop publishes.

## Ownership

The Treehouse name, logo, wordmark, and visual identity are owned by Pixelhop.
Nothing in the AGPL-3.0 license, and nothing in these guidelines, transfers
ownership of those marks.

## What you can do without asking

- Run, self-host, modify, and redistribute the Treehouse source code under the
  AGPL-3.0, including for commercial purposes.
- State, truthfully and without implying endorsement, that your product "is
  built on Treehouse", "is powered by Treehouse", or "is compatible with
  Treehouse".
- Use the name "Treehouse" in blog posts, talks, tutorials, and documentation
  that refer to this project.
- Keep the Treehouse name and marks in unmodified copies you redistribute.

## What requires a different name

If you **fork Treehouse and distribute a modified version**, or run a modified
version as a public service, you must **not** use the Treehouse name or logo in
a way that could confuse users into thinking your fork is the official project
or is endorsed by Pixelhop. In particular:

- Do not name your fork, product, company, or hosted service "Treehouse" (or a
  confusingly similar variation), and do not use the Treehouse logo as your
  product's logo.
- Do not use "Treehouse" in a domain name, social media handle, or app store
  listing for a modified version or a competing hosted offering.
- Do not use the Treehouse marks in any way that suggests Pixelhop sponsors,
  endorses, or is affiliated with your fork.

Pick your own distinct name and branding for a fork. You are still free to note
factually that it is "based on Treehouse".

## Hosted service

Pixelhop offers a managed, hosted Treehouse cloud. Running your own hosted
instance of unmodified Treehouse is fine, but you may not brand a competing
hosted service as "Treehouse" or imply it is the official Pixelhop service.

## Questions

These guidelines are intentionally close to those used by other AGPL projects
(e.g. Plausible Analytics). If you are unsure whether a particular use is
permitted, or you would like permission for a use not covered here, contact
Pixelhop at <info@pixelhop.io>.
