There are two ways to let someone in. **Members** join the whole workspace and can read, write, comment and connect their own agents. **Anyone with a link** can read one file or folder you choose, and nothing else. Most teams use both: members for the people doing the work, share links for clients and readers.

Once people are in, comments and the activity feed keep everyone, human or agent, on the same page.
